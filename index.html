Common questions

Where is my progress saved?On your device, inside the app. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
Do I have to watch ads?No. Ads only play when you choose a reward in the Bonus menu and tap Watch ad.
How do I change ad tracking?In iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, you can also use Ad privacy choices in the game's Settings.
My custom sound didn't load.Make sure the video has audio, then try again. Very long or unusual videos can take a few seconds to process.
